How these templates work

A PowerScribe AutoText .rtf holds two things: the report prose you see, and — appended after the RTF — an XML block defining every field, its pick list, and its character offset into the report text.

Pick lists

Each choice has a label (the short word shown in the template and spoken to select it) and a statement (the sentence actually inserted into the report). A choice with a blank label shows its statement as the label — that is how PowerScribe stores it, and this editor keeps that behaviour.

The default choice is the one pre-filled when the template loads; in the report body it is the one drawn in red.

Offsets

Those offsets are why hand-editing these files breaks them: change one character of prose and every field after it points at the wrong place. This editor recomputes all of them on save, and refuses to save if they do not line up.

Good to know

  • Labels and field names cannot contain : or / — those separate fields from choices.
  • A field deleted from the report body is dropped from the XML on save.
  • Ctrl/⌘+S saves, Ctrl+O opens, Enter splits a paragraph.
  • Nothing leaves this computer — no network calls, no uploads.
